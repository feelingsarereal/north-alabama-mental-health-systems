**What this is.** A public, searchable, interactive edition of the *North Alabama Mental Health Systems Report* (Little Orange Fish, prepared with Claude): the regional companion to the statewide site you built as K7. It describes the mental health system in Madison and Cullman counties, which are WellStone's service area, and the fifteen-county catchment of the Huntsville crisis center. Unlike the statewide report, nobody handed over a manuscript: the report was researched and written for this site from public sources, and every sentence was then read back against the passage that supports it. The site has five jobs:

1. **Read the report**, with the source of every sentence one hover away: a summary of the source and the passage that supports the sentence.
2. **See what could not be settled**: 129 open items, each with where it was looked for and what would close it.
3. **Explore the region on two maps**: the fifteen counties (36 layers), and the 117 census tracts of Madison and Cullman (28 layers) with the service locations the report names.
4. **Explore the system as a graph**: the statewide report's twelve parts plus the ring, the eleven dependencies this report draws between them, and the Madison, Cullman and Alabama figures behind each.
5. **Learn the ideas**: 12 primers and a 189-term glossary written for a reader with no background in health policy.

It runs under `mode: topic` (the report is `review.md`) with the **same schema extensions as the statewide app**, specified normatively in §4b–4g below: `claims-ledger.yaml`, `open-items.yaml`, `geo/`, `systems/indicators.json`, `brand/`, and block-id comments in `review.md`. There is **no `changes.yaml`, no `/changes` route and no US map** in this app: there was no earlier draft to compare with, and the benchmark here is Alabama, not the other states. `docs/` was not edited.

**The numbers:** about 21,200 words of prose in 126 sections and 283 blocks; 407 references (336 read in full, 70 in part, 1 that could not be opened and is cited only for that fact); 1,497 ledger entries (978 verified against a quoted passage, 234 the report's own analysis or arithmetic, 180 dated "not found" statements, 105 statements about the report itself); 129 open items; 189 glossary terms; 12 primers; 6 figures; 65 map layers; 54 service locations. ## 1. Hard rules

- **Never invent content.** Every sentence, definition, summary, number and label a reader sees comes from `content-pack/`. A gap renders as an amber `TODO(author)` marker (`.bx-todo`) and lists on `/methods`. This applies with full force to the maps: **no value, rank, average, total or colour class may be shown that is not a cell of the pack's CSVs or computed from them by a rule stated in §4d**, and anything computed is labelled as computed.
- **Body text is rendered as written.** `review.md` is the report as checked on 4 October 2026. Structure it, link it, render its tables. Never rewrite, shorten or "fix" it. If you believe a sentence is wrong, put it in your scrum note.
- **Uncited prose fails the build** (APP-SPEC §6.1): any block of 25 words or more in `review.md` with no `[n]` and no `<!-- framing -->` marker is a content-build error. The same rule holds for primer bodies. Do not repair one by adding a citation.
- **`<!-- synthesis -->` blocks stay visible** (there are 59, chiefly the "Gaps and opportunities" lists). Render each with the quiet left rule and the label **"analysis"**, give each an id, and list them on `/methods`.
- **A citation is only as good as its ledger entry.** The hover in §4b shows the passage the check recorded. If no ledger entry in the block has a source for a reference number that appears in the sentence, show the reference summary alone; never synthesise a "supporting passage".
- **A broken claim anchor fails loudly.** Every ledger entry must be found in its block by the rule in §4b. Zero matches, or more than one, is a content-build error listed in `BUILD-ERRORS.md` and on `/methods`. Do not re-point it at a similar sentence.
- **Nothing implies approval, peer review or WellStone's involvement.** The banner (§4f) is on every route and cannot be dismissed. `manifest.venue` is rendered on `/` and `/about`. `as_of` (2026-10-04) appears on `/`, `/read`, `/about`, both maps and `/systems`. WellStone's name appears only as the report uses it: never in the site title, the repository description beyond "WellStone's service area", a logo, or any phrase that suggests it produced, reviewed or endorsed the site.
- **Uncertainty is part of the value.** A tract estimate is never shown without its margin of error (§4d); a modeled estimate is always labelled modeled; an approximate map marker is always labelled approximate. Do not hide these behind a click.
- **No runtime fetches.** No tiles, no geometry, no fonts, no analytics, nothing fetched at runtime. County geometry is bundled from `us-atlas`; tract geometry is fetched **once, at build time, by a script**, converted and committed (§4d). Fonts are self-hosted.
- **Fail loudly in the content build.** Schema violation, unresolved `[n]`, unknown term/concept/section/block id, missing data file, a map column with no `layers.json` entry or the reverse, a FIPS code or tract id with no geometry, a ledger source number with no reference, an open item pointing at an unknown block → non-zero exit with a clear list. Write `content-pack/BUILD-ERRORS.md` and continue with whatever validated.
- **Delivery mode is `pages`**, GitHub account **`feelingsarereal`** (§6). `gh auth status` must show that account as active, or you stop. Never push this app to any other account.
- Vault contract: touch nothing outside this folder except K8's `state`; `notes/`, `source/` and the 🚀 hub note are excluded from the repo via `.git/info/exclude`.

## 4b. The reader's source layer — normative
